Bestiary: what lives in the Lampway

Draft. Every monster family in the game (src/bestiary.js), where it came from and how it fits the world, then the named uniques and the Lantern-Eater. Depths are where each kind first appears, in feet. The rings are described in The Lampway.

Where the monsters come from

Nothing in the Deep was put there to fight delvers. Everything there is there for one of seven reasons:

  1. Native. Creatures of the dark rock that were there before the Keepers, or that came up from the deep fires.
  2. Come down. Hill creatures, beasts and people that moved into the empty halls after the Fall.
  3. Made. Things people built or bred: golems, the old royal menagerie's hybrids, gargoyles, mimics.
  4. The dead. Those buried in the Ring of Rest whose last spark could not rise once the lamps went out.
  5. The fire. Spirits and dragons of the deep fires, loose since the Ashborn left the Ring of Forges.
  6. The dark. Scraps of Morrowgloom itself, and things from the Unlit Ring below it.
  7. The light. Lost sparks: wisps that wandered off from a lamp, or starlight caught in the Ring of Glass.
