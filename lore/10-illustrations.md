# Illustrations

*A list of illustrations for the lore, with a prompt for each. The look follows three reference pictures: an old
adventure-module page in pen and ink, a loose ink-wash scene, and a coloured comic-ink character. None of the
references is copied; they only set the style.*

## How to use the prompts

Each illustration names a **style** (A, B or C). Write the prompt as: **subject text, then the style block**. Every
subject is written from the lore, so the creatures and people are original designs; keep the last line of each
style block ("original design...") in the prompt, since it keeps generators from drifting towards famous
characters.

Suggested sizes: portraits 2:3 (for example 1024 × 1536), scenes and places 16:9 (1536 × 864), items 1:1. Save the
results in `lore/art/` with the file names given here.

## The three styles

**Style A: pen and ink (old module page).**
> Black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration in the manner of a 1980s fantasy adventure-module page: fine crosshatching,
> stippled shadows, bold black areas, white paper showing through as highlights, hand-drawn line weight, slight
> paper texture. Dramatic light from one source (a torch or lamp), deep black shadows. No colour, no gradients, no
> text. Original design, not based on any existing game, film or book character.

**Style B: ink wash (field sketch).**
> Loose black ink and grey ink-wash illustration on warm off-white paper: fast expressive brushstrokes, dry-brush
> texture, spattered ink, heavy black shadow masses, a few sharp pen lines for detail, figures small against big dark
> spaces. Monochrome, eerie and atmospheric, like a sketch made by torchlight. No text. Original design, not based
> on any existing game, film or book character.

**Style C: coloured comic ink (character plate).**
> European comic-book illustration: confident black ink outlines with hatching in the shadows, flat and slightly
> textured colour like watercolour or gouache, limited warm palette (ochre, rust, teal, dusty pink) with one strong
> accent colour, low-angle heroic composition, characterful and a little humorous, detailed costume and gear. No
> text. Original design, not based on any existing game, film or book character.

Use **A** for monsters, crypts and dread; **B** for places, legends and moments of mystery; **C** for the peoples,
heroes, shopkeepers and anything with a personality.
