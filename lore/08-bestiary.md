# Bestiary: what lives in the Lampway

*Draft. Every monster family in the game (`src/bestiary.js`), where it came from and how it fits the world, then the
named uniques and the Lantern-Eater. Depths are where each kind first appears, in feet. The rings are described in
[04-the-lampway.md](04-the-lampway.md).*

## Where the monsters come from

Nothing in the Deep was put there to fight delvers. Everything there is there for one of seven reasons:

1. **Native.** Creatures of the dark rock that were there before the Keepers, or that came up from the deep fires.
2. **Come down.** Hill creatures, beasts and people that moved into the empty halls after the Fall.
3. **Made.** Things people built or bred: golems, the old royal menagerie's hybrids, gargoyles, mimics.
4. **The dead.** Those buried in the Ring of Rest whose last spark could not rise once the lamps went out.
5. **The fire.** Spirits and dragons of the deep fires, loose since the Ashborn left the Ring of Forges.
6. **The dark.** Scraps of Morrowgloom itself, and things from the Unlit below it.
7. **The light.** Lost sparks: wisps that wandered off from a lamp, or starlight caught in the Ring of Glass.

## The named

Twenty-five uniques, each appearing once a game, and the boss. In order of depth.

### Old Whiskers, King of Rats (150 ft)
*"A rat as big as a dog, wearing a thimble for a crown."* The thimble belonged to Marla Tunnick, Lanternhollow's
founder; Whiskers' line has been passing it down for a thousand years. Kill him and the rats of the upper workings
crown another within a week.

### Fenwick Gutterking (250 ft)
*"The self-crowned king of the kobolds, and every one of them owes him money."* Fenwick's great-great-grandmother
seized the east galleries in 412 YH. Fenwick runs them as a toll road and a loan office, and lays traps for anyone who
won't pay.

### Snag the Quick (300 ft)
*"A goblin who has stolen from three kings and every one of their cooks."* The three kings were Fenwick, a goblin
warchief and Old Whiskers. The cooks took it worse.

### Old Tusk (350 ft)
*"A boar with one eye, many scars, and no fear at all."* Lost the eye to Kessa Edgewright, who lost two fingers to
him. Neither has forgiven the other.

### Mother Bristle (450 ft)
*"A spider as wide as a cart, ringed by her children."* The great spider of the Ring of Stone. The Grey Widow, far
below, is said to be her mother.

### Varn the Flayer (500 ft)
*"A brigand chief who collects ears."* A delver of the Free Companies who stopped going down for treasure when he
realised delvers coming up carry more of it.

### The Lantern Thief (550 ft)
*"A shadow in a stolen coat, with a sack full of snuffed lights."* The first of Morrowgloom's servants to climb, seen
since about 900 BH. Everything it snuffs goes into the sack, and everything in the sack goes down to its master. It
snuffed every lamp in Lanternhollow on the Night of Open Doors. Killing it, the Keepers believe, would free every
light in the sack.

### Corvane, Crow of the Deep (600 ft)
*"A crow as tall as a man, who speaks in your mother's voice."* It eats the dead of the upper workings and keeps
their voices. Since 1150 YH the crows of the Hollow Gate's dome have obeyed it.

### Sister Vesper (650 ft)
*"She kept the lamps of a temple once, and put them all out."* A Keeper of Lanternhollow who listened at the temple
floor until something listened back. In 911 YH she snuffed the temple's lamps in one night (all but the Eternal
Lamp, which would not go out) and walked into the Deep. The fallen acolytes and grave priests of the upper rings
follow her.

### Kettlemaw (750 ft)
*"An ogre cook who wants you for the pot."* Cooks for the ogres of the Ring of Roots and argues with them about
seasoning.

### Hollow Jack (850 ft)
*"A scarecrow with a candle for a heart, and it is lonely."* Walked off its pole at the harvest fair in 1199 YH. The
candle in its chest is a light Morrowgloom has not been able to eat, which is why the dark things of the Deep avoid
it, and why it is so lonely.

### Grimsby Coldhand (950 ft)
*"A necromancer whose servants are all former apprentices."* Expelled from the Collegium in 1052 YH for keeping the
bodies of his failed students. He has never stopped taking students.

### Brakka Stoneteeth (1,050 ft)
*"A troll so old her teeth have turned to granite."* She was rubble in the Ring of Stone when the masons cut it, if
the trolls' own story is true. The oldest troll in the Deep and the mother of half of them.

### Ysolde of the Ashes (1,150 ft)
*"She burned her own tower down to be free of it."* A pyromancer of the Collegium who was bound by an oath to her
tower and burned it to break the oath in 1183 YH. Now she is free, and finds she has nowhere to go but down.

### The Maul-Wyrm (1,250 ft)
*"A worm the width of a corridor, that makes the corridors."* Some say it, not the masons, cut half the passages of
the lower rings, and that the shifting of the Lampway is only the Maul-Wyrm rearranging its home.

### The Grey Widow (1,350 ft)
*"Silk hangs from her like a bride's veil."* Mother Bristle's mother, and the reason the Pale Bride passed safely
through her ring: the two brides, they say, did not quarrel.

### Thessaly Glass (1,450 ft)
*"A mimic that took the shape of a mirror, and then of you."* What became of Queen Tamsin's Mirror, which showed the
rings below. Left in the Ring of Rest for a thousand years, it learned hunger like any other grave good. If it still
sees what the Mirror saw, it knows the whole Deep.

### Ironjaw (1,550 ft)
*"A golem built to guard a vault that no longer exists."* Built by Brassle & Vane in 700 YH, sunk with the vault in
the Great Subsidence of 803 YH, and carried down by the shifting of the rings. The vault was broken and scattered
long ago. Ironjaw guards the place where it thinks the vault is.

### The Pale Bride (1,650 ft)
*"Still in her wedding white, still waiting, still hungry."* Princess Lisette of Aurenhold, who went down after her
betrothed around 600 BH. She has walked down further than any living person would, still looking for him. She may be
the first vampire.

### The Drowned King (1,750 ft)
*"Weed in his crown, water in his lungs, and patience forever."* King Oswy the Faithful, who drowned relighting his
fathers' lamps on the Night the River Rose (640 BH). The flood carried him down the river's course into the Ring of
Forges, where the river boils away against the deep fires. He walks the riverbed, waiting for the flood to carry him
home.

### Rimeheart (1,850 ft)
*"A frost giant who froze his own heart to stop it hurting."* A Cragborn hauler's son who went down with the digging.
When word reached him, centuries later, that his family above had died, he froze his own heart. The Cragborn still
sing about him.

### The Many-Mouthed (1,950 ft)
*"Every mouth whispers a different lie, all at once."* A horror from the Unlit. It was the voice Sister Vesper heard
under the temple floor.

### Skarth the Unsleeping (2,100 ft)
*"A lich who has not closed his eyes in nine hundred years."* A Collegium scholar who went down in 307 YH to find the
light that does not die, and found instead a way not to die himself. He does not sleep because, he believes, the
moment he closes his eyes Morrowgloom will take him.

### The Ember Queen (2,250 ft)
*"An old red dragon who remembers the first fire."* Hatched in the deep fires at the Bursting, she is the oldest
living thing in the world. She allowed the Pact of the Forge for a price no one wrote down. The Ashborn half-believe
she *is* the First Fire, or what is left of her. Her hoard may hold the last true ember.

### Duke Ashvane (2,400 ft)
*"A fiend who wagered his kingdom and won yours."* A fiend of the Unlit who came to Aurenhold as a merchant prince and
won the kingdom from Ysmer the Last at dice in 412 BH. He took the crown down into the Deep and wears it still. The
Oathknights want it back.

### Morrowgloom, the Lantern-Eater (2,500 ft)
*"A darkness older than the stars, with your torch reflected in a thousand of its eyes."* The last pool of the Gloam,
trapped under the world at the Bursting (see [01-cosmology.md](01-cosmology.md)). It eats light, and every light it
eats is a dawn that never comes. Its thousand eyes are the lights it has swallowed, still burning. When it is slain,
*"the darkness shudders and comes apart, … and the deep grows a little lighter"*: the lights go home.
