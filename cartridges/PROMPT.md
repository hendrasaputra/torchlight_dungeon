# Cover art prompt

Id: `torchlight-dungeons`

> Scene: a lone adventurer holding up a flickering torch in a dark stone dungeon, seen from above at a slight angle,
> drawn as ASCII characters on a grid. The torch casts a warm orange circle of light on the rough floor and the
> wall stones around them; beyond it everything fades to black, except the dim blue outline of corridors they
> have already walked. At the edge of the light a green goblin with a rusty blade steps forward, and further off a
> small cold-blue glowing wisp lights its own patch of the wall. A staircase leads down in the corner. A few
> bright bits of a defeated rat tumble across the floor. The adventurer and creatures are original designs, not
> based on any existing game or book. Mood: tense, mysterious, adventurous.

`torchlight-dungeons.jpg` was made from this prompt for the arcade shelf, when the game was still drawn in ASCII
characters. Since phase 6 the game is drawn in code-made pixel art; the prompt below is for new art in that look.

## Pixel art (phase 10)

> Scene: crisp 16-bit style pixel art, a three-quarter top-down view of a dark stone dungeon. A lone adventurer in a
> padded jerkin, short sword in one hand, holds up a flickering torch; its warm orange light spills over flagstone
> floor tiles and the brick faces of the walls nearby, and fades softly into black. Remembered corridors beyond the
> light show as dim, cold blue. On the left a vein of rust-red magma rock with flecks of gold runs through the wall;
> a pile of grey rubble half blocks a corridor. At the edge of the light a green goblin with tusks and a rusty blade
> steps forward, and further off a small pale-blue wisp lights its own patch of wall. A stone staircase leads down in
> a corner, and a faint glowing rune is set into the floor. Chunky pixels, a dark one-pixel outline on every figure,
> no smoothing. The title "TORCHLIGHT DUNGEONS" in a warm, blocky pixel font across the top. All characters and
> creatures are original designs, not from any existing game or book. Mood: tense, mysterious, inviting.
